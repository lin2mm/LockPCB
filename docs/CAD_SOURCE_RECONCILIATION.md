### What the files say or depict

- The designer-confirmed TXT specifies a **single 18500 lithium cell**, **3.3 V motor**, right-hand door as default with a left/right selection procedure, button-operated lock/unlock, blue indicator LED and buzzer feedback, Bluetooth app plus Bluetooth keypad, and lock/unlock motor stall as a signal. It also specifies the two fixing holes as positive/negative battery contacts. These are confirmed facts about the factory-prototype package; exact motor MPN/stall current, detailed electrical implementation and safety validation are still open.
- The current STEP parse gives the node named `DC-18650-SX` a mesh envelope of **18.00 × 17.994 × 50.00 mm**. If it is the modeled cell, this geometry is 18500-sized despite the 18650 name, and therefore supports the TXT's 18500 specification. Another battery-associated node, `SX-AZB-18650`, measures 35.50 × 17.501 × 85.549 mm and is not identified as a bare cell. The earlier user-confirmed single-18650 product requirement remains in conflict with this factory-prototype evidence; actual sample/BOM identity must close the issue.
- The confirmed PCB dimension JPG visibly calls out **14.00 mm × 50.00 mm**. DWG parsing and STEP mesh bounds independently agree on that plan size; the DWG also calls out **1.20 mm** thickness and **2 × Ø3.10 mm** end holes. The DWG end-hole center pitch is 37 mm, with 3 mm and 10 mm end offsets; the 28 mm JPG callout is reconcilable to the DWG feature coordinates in the matching board orientation. A separate DWG 5.50 mm datum is not yet proven to be the same feature as the JPG's 5.00 mm. The Agent KiCad outline has now been adjusted from 17.5 × 60 mm test geometry to the DWG-derived 14 × 50 mm notched outline; visual rings mark the two source holes but do not yet define drills/copper pads. Because the DXF conversion warned and reports inch units, verify against a clean native-mm export before fabrication. Full caveats are in `docs/FACTORY_PROTOTYPE_GEOMETRY_REPORT.md`; this does not establish fit to the assembled housing.
- The STEP header identifies `SXS-ZP-4_ASM`, Creo Parametric, 2026-07-29, with millimetre units. Its parsed `SX-PCB-5` mesh bounds are 14.00 × 5.70 × 50.00 mm (the 5.70 mm is the modeled whole-board/component envelope, not bare laminate thickness); `SX-DB-4` bounds are 39.80 × 22.50 × 90.494 mm. The STEP also has several cover/assembly variants (`SX-DC-GAI-4`, `SX-DC-DDK`, `SX-DCG-JCP`, and subassemblies); do not assume every named variant is installed simultaneously or is the released revision. The parsed PCB/cell AABB has only 0.6 mm separation on one axis, which flags a tight stack but is not a surface clearance/interference test. See `docs/FACTORY_PROTOTYPE_GEOMETRY_REPORT.md`; no full CAD fit has been validated.
- The DWG has an AutoCAD `AC1032` file signature and has since been converted to DXF and parsed. Conversion warned `DWG_ERR_VALUEOUTOFBOUNDS`, and its header advertises inches while the dimension geometry cross-checks to millimetres; rely on the cross-confirmed key dimensions, retain the unit/conversion caveat for other dimensions, and obtain a clean native-mm export before CAD migration.

## Reconciliation with the current PCB baseline

The previously confirmed project brief specifies a compact indoor retrofit lock with one vertical 1S 18650 alongside a long PCB and motor/gearing/coupler at the top. The designer-confirmed factory-prototype package specifies one 18500 in its TXT; the STEP node named `DC-18650-SX` is modeled at 18 × 18 × 50 mm, supporting 18500-size geometry and suggesting a stale node label. Confirm the physical cell/sample BOM before fixing the product-level SKU. The approved board is 14 × 50 mm. The factory STEP provides a 39.8 × 22.5 × 90.494 mm shell mesh matching the old standalone STEP, but the older FCStd assembly still differs and no source alone confirms usable internal PCB clearance. Wall thickness, battery/contact space, cover travel, motor/gearbox, shaft datum, service access, metal and RF exclusions still consume volume.

Therefore:

1. Keep all mandatory named zones in `pcb/LockPCB.kicad_pcb`; do not replace `TBD` dimensions with the historical external envelope.
2. Use the source-derived 14 × 50 mm notched Edge.Cuts outline now in KiCad as the Agent's mechanical baseline for iteration; it is not by itself a fit/fabrication approval. Retire the 17.5 × 60 / 20 × 65 test envelopes from product geometry.
3. Do not reverse-engineer board placement from screenshots or old non-authoritative plastic STEP variants.
4. Confirm the factory sample/BOM battery SKU (the 18 × 50 mm STEP cell geometry supports 18500 despite its 18650 label). Verify the imported outline/coordinate transform against a clean native-mm CAD export; then implement the two Ø3.10 mm drills/contact pads after polarity/plating approval. Match the selected designer-confirmed enclosure assembly to the user's sampled/assembled shell and validate PCB/cell/contact/motor placement, shaft axis, RF/NFC exclusions, cover travel, service access and tolerance stack in one coordinate system. Detailed parsed dimensions and caveats: `docs/FACTORY_PROTOTYPE_GEOMETRY_REPORT.md`.
5. Keep battery model, holder/contact, actual internal free volume, motor specifications, and board-to-shell fit as `TBD` until verified.
